Operations

The floor plan is the golem's own map, released into its journal. Click a room to entrust a mission to that point, or send it to a place by name: the golem plans the shortest road through the doors and open boundaries, journals it, and journals every passage it crosses.

Let go: the golem lets go of every mission — journaled as g.Retire(reason), nothing is erased — and the body is put back. Reset everything (lab lever): wipes the journals of this golem AND its peers and reboots them reborn at entry 1 — both, because the hearer dedups the sender's tell ids.

PerformQuery

—

Queries read the golem's in-memory state and never touch the journal. Keep them read-only: a mutating call here would change memory without journaling it, and the change evaporates on the next rehydration. Distances follow the map's passages: g.Distance('kitchen', 'garage') is the shortest road through the doors, and the header's route left · eta comes from /progress, which only adds the road from where the body stands.

Journal — live

    Everything the journal receives, record by record, as it lands: the release chain, each define action (the template), each action (the template with its arguments), the tells, the acks and the verdicts. The tag at the right is the record kind. At boot the whole diary is replayed here first.

    Runtime events

      Ephemeral facts (roads chosen, legs started, holds, membrane, lifecycle) — they never touch the journal.
