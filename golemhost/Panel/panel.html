Operations

Click the map to entrust a mission. A target the body cannot reach ends as a journaled Fail (stuck against a wall after 3 s without progress, or timeout).

The golem lets go of every mission — journaled as g.Retire(reason), nothing is erased — and the body is put back (turtle to the center, trails cleared).

PerformQuery

—

Queries read the golem's in-memory state and never touch the journal. Keep them read-only: a mutating call here would change memory without journaling it, and the change evaporates on the next rehydration.

Journal — live

    Every row is a journaled fact, projected by one of the golem's view reactions (Assigned, Taken, Completed, Failed, Retired, Acked) with the entry that triggered it.

    Runtime events

      Ephemeral facts (missions starting, membrane, lifecycle) — they never touch the journal.
