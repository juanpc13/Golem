Operations

Click the map to entrust a mission. A target the body cannot reach ends as a journaled Fail (stuck against a wall after 3 s without progress, or timeout).

Let go: the golem lets go of every mission — journaled as g.Retire(reason), nothing is erased — and the body is put back. Reset everything (lab lever): wipes the journals of this golem AND its peers and reboots them reborn at entry 1 — both, because the hearer dedups the sender's tell ids.

PerformQuery

—

Queries read the golem's in-memory state and never touch the journal. Keep them read-only: a mutating call here would change memory without journaling it, and the change evaporates on the next rehydration. The header's route left · eta is the golem's own answer too: it knows its speed and its pauses (released into its journal), so g.RouteSeconds() answers from the console with no parameters; /progress only adds the leg from where the body stands (its pose, as a query parameter) to the first pending point.

Journal — live

    Everything the journal receives, record by record, as it lands: the release chain, each define action (the template), each action (the template with its arguments), the tells, the acks and the verdicts. The tag at the right is the record kind. At boot the whole diary is replayed here first.

    Runtime events

      Ephemeral facts (missions starting, holds, membrane, lifecycle) — they never touch the journal.
